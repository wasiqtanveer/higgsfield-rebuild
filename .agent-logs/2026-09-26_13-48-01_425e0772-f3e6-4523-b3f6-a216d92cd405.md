**The real problem: there was no exit animation.** Only the incoming page animated. The page you were leaving was destroyed on the same frame you clicked — so what you actually saw was a hard cut, then a fade onto an empty screen. That reads as a glitch, and no amount of tuning the entrance could fix it, because nothing was leaving.

**What I changed:**

Both halves now move, deliberately asymmetric ([RouteTransition.jsx](src/components/RouteTransition/RouteTransition.jsx)):
- **Out** — the old page dims and lifts away over 160ms on an accelerating curve. It's already decided, so it gathers speed rather than easing to a stop.
- **In** — the new page rises 22px into place over 300ms on a decelerating curve.

`mode="wait"` sequences them so they never overlap — cross-fading two full pages means a tall one shows through a short one.

I also made the motion bigger than before. The old values (10px, no exit) were sized to play *under* the interstitial; once that was removed they were far too subtle to notice.
