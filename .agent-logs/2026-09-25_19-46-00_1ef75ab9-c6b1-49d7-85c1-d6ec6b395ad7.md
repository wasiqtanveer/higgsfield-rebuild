# The section: `#feed` — "One prompt, yours to change"

Written for: you, plus the agents who'll build the parallel sections.

## Why the ledger failed

Diagnosis worth keeping, because it's the trap every one of these sections falls into: I had six rows each showing a *different* prompt. Six is a list, and a list is something you skim and leave. It also meant the visitor never *did* anything — the entire interaction was hover-to-brighten, which is not an interaction, it's a highlight. Six weak demonstrations of an idea are worse than one real one.

## The thesis

**One prompt. One image. The prompt is editable, and the image answers.**

The section refuses the category's grid. Instead of showing many finished things, it shows a single artifact and hands the visitor the controls. The claim "the prompt is the artifact" stops being a headline and becomes something they just did with their own hands, in about four seconds.

## First viewport (fixed, one screen, never grows)

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  THE FEED                                                    │
│  Every image here          ┌────────────────────┐            │
│  is someone's prompt.      │                    │            │
│                            │   the generation   │            │
│  a woman walking down      │   (real still,     │            │
│  a manhattan avenue,       │    portrait,       │            │

│                                                              │
│  mira · 214 forks · 3 deep          Open in the composer →    │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

- Prompt **left**, large mono, full measure. It is the biggest text in the section.
- Image **right**, portrait, roughly 40% of the width — deliberately *not* the hero of the layout.
- One line of the prompt is the **active line**, marked in accent with a caret.
- Below, **three chips**: real alternative phrasings for that line. Clicking one rewrites the line and the image responds.

## The signature interaction

Click a chip → three things happen at once, on one shared 420ms curve:

1. **The line retypes.** Old words strike through and evaporate; new words type in at uneven cadence. The accent travels with the change.
2. **The image responds by degree** — never by swapping photo. The same still shifts crop, scale and grade: "at her true scale" is wide and cold, "as a miniature" pushes in tight and warms the grade, "lit from below" drops the exposure and lifts contrast. Implemented as CSS `transform` + `filter` on one element, so it's GPU-cheap and *honest* — it is visibly the same photograph being reinterpreted, which is exactly what the constraint permits.
3. **The fork counter ticks up by one** and a thin accent thread draws from the old line to the new one — the lineage made literal, from real data, with no fake sibling image.

That's the whole product argument, performed. And it satisfies the no-fake-lineage constraint precisely: we never claim two images are forks of each other, because there is only ever one image.

## What carries the immersion

The previous version's motion budget was one opacity transition. This one:

- **Entry**: the prompt lines stagger in from a mask (reuse the hero's `Title` grammar so the two sections read as one system); the image arrives from behind with the `Reveal` `depth` variant; the chips pop last.
- **Idle**: the active line carries a slow accent breathe — barely perceptible, enough that the section is never dead.
- **Ambient**: a very low-opacity `PhotoWall` fragment drifts behind the image plate only, clipped to it — so the section sits on the product's own output without repeating the hero's full-bleed wall.
- **The pointer**: `Magnetic` on the chips, and the image plate takes a subtle parallax tilt toward the cursor.
- **Exit**: nothing. It's one viewport; it leaves by scrolling.

Every one of these is gated on `prefers-reduced-motion`, which under that setting shows the composed end state with the first chip selected and no motion at all.

## Boundaries

- **Untouched**: the Header pill, the Hero, tokens.css, the `useSectionInFocus` observer in Home.jsx.
- **Anti-goals**: no masonry grid. No second photo wall at full bleed. No carousel. No second CTA competing with the composer link. Accent stays rationed to the active line, the thread, and the fork tick — nothing else.
