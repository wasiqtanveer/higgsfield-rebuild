## The idea, in one line

**The prompt is the artifact, not the image.**

Every tool in this category shows you a finished picture and throws away the recipe. Graft stores the *prompt* as the thing that matters — the image is just what it produced this time. That makes prompts **forkable like code**: open someone's image, change one line, run it again, and your version becomes a child with the diff against its parent visible. Prompts form a tree.

**Lineage is the feature the category doesn't have.** Hence the name — a graft is a branch joined onto an existing tree. It's also why `/create` has a seed field: pinning the seed is what makes a fork comparable to its parent instead of to noise.

## "New root prompt" — you were right

That was database vocabulary leaking into the UI. Nobody writing their first prompt thinks "I am creating a root node." Replaced with:

> **STARTING FRESH**
> Nothing above this one yet. Whatever you write becomes something other people can fork.

Says the same thing, explains the product while it does it. The fork case now reads **"Growing from"** rather than "Forking".
